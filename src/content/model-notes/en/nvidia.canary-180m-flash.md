## Overview
NVIDIA NeMo Canary 180M Flash is a compact four-language recognition and translation model, supporting bidirectional speech translation between English, Spanish, German, and French.

## Use cases
- Transcription in English, Spanish, German, and French
- Translate foreign-language speech directly to English text, or vice versa
- Lightweight multilingual demos and prototypes

## Languages and capabilities
- Languages: English, Spanish, German, and French
- Speech recognition + speech translation

## Usage tips
- INT8 is about 210 MB and loads quickly.
- Covers only four languages. For others, use Parakeet or SenseVoice.
