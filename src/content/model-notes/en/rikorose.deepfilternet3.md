## Overview
DeepFilterNet3 performs full-band (48 kHz) speech denoising, suppressing broadband noise while preserving voice detail. It is the default recommendation for recording cleanup.

## Use cases
- Postproduction cleanup for podcasts, interviews, and meetings
- Preprocess audio before recognition or voice cloning
- Denoise voice messages and microphone recordings

## Languages and capabilities
- Language-independent
- Full-band processing for high-sample-rate recordings
- Preserves duration and supports segmented long-audio processing

## Usage tips
- Downloads the official platform runtime (deep-filter) during installation.
- For mixed vocals and music, separate with Spleeter before denoising.
