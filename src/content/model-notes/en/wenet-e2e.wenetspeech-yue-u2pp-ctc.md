## Overview
A WeNet U2++ Conformer model trained on WeNetSpeech-Yue for Cantonese recognition, also covering Mandarin and English.

## Use cases
- Transcribe Cantonese videos, podcasts, and interviews
- Meeting notes for Hong Kong and Macao
- Near-real-time recognition with VAD segmentation

## Languages and capabilities
- Languages: Cantonese, Chinese, and English
- CTC decoding with timestamps

## Usage tips
- Use VAD segmentation for long audio.
- For Mandarin-only content, consider SenseVoice or Paraformer first.
