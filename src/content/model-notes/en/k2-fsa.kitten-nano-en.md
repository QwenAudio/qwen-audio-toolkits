## Overview
KittenTTS Nano is a 30 MB English multi-voice synthesis model. It needs no reference audio and can start reading within seconds.

## Use cases
- English prompts and quick reading
- English TTS on resource-limited devices
- Prototypes and voice previews

## Languages and capabilities
- Language: English
- Multiple preset voices

## Usage tips
- Prioritizes lightweight operation. For production voiceovers, consider SupertonicTTS or cloud models.
