## Overview
Model Studio Qwen-Audio 3.0 ASR Flash Streaming targets meetings, support, and live captions, with multiple languages, semantic segmentation, context enhancement, and word timestamps.

## Use cases
- Live captions and meeting interpretation
- Real-time support-agent assistance
- Live streams and voice conversations

## Languages and capabilities
- Multilingual
- Streaming recognition, semantic segmentation, word timestamps, and context enhancement

## Usage tips
- Maintain a stable network connection for microphone capture.
- For 8 kHz telephone audio, choose the 8K Realtime version.
