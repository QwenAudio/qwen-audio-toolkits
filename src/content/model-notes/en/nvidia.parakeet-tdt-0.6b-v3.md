## Overview
NVIDIA NeMo Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 offers high-quality offline recognition across 25 European languages, with word timestamps.

## Use cases
- English and European-language meetings and podcasts
- Caption alignment with word timestamps
- Multilingual European content

## Languages and capabilities
- 25 European languages, including English, German, French, Spanish, and Italian
- TDT decoding with word timing

## Usage tips
- INT8 is about 650 MB.
- Does not support Chinese. For Asian languages, use SenseVoice / Fun-ASR.
