## Overview
Fun-ASR Nano is the official local runtime for FunAudioLLM's large-model recognition approach. Built-in FSMN-VAD segmentation and the official GGUF inference pipeline support reliable long-audio recognition.

## Use cases
- Interviews and meetings featuring Chinese dialects
- Mixed Chinese, English, and Japanese content
- High-quality offline transcription of long audio

## Languages and capabilities
- Languages: Chinese, English, Japanese, and multiple Chinese dialects
- Built-in automatic VAD segmentation for long audio

## Usage tips
- Q4_K_M / Q5_K_M / Q8 variants are about 0.9–1.2 GB. Choose according to available memory.
- Large-model inference is slower. For quick short-clip transcription, use SenseVoice.
