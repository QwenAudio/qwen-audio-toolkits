## Overview
Useful Sensors' Moonshine v2 Tiny is a compact, fast English recognition model optimized for short speech and devices with limited resources.

## Use cases
- English voice commands and short-phrase transcription
- English recognition on low-spec devices
- Quick dictation of English notes

## Languages and capabilities
- Language: English
- Optimized for short audio

## Usage tips
- Use VAD segmentation for long audio.
- For Chinese or multilingual audio, use SenseVoice / Qwen3-ASR.
