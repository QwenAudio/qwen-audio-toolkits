## Overview
MyShell.ai's MeloTTS is a natural-sounding Chinese and English model supporting mixed-language text, and a common choice for local Chinese TTS.

## Use cases
- Narration, podcast intros, and reading content
- Voice responses for conversational assistants
- Reading mixed Chinese and English text

## Languages and capabilities
- Languages: Chinese and English, including mixed text
- Single speaker with adjustable speed

## Usage tips
- Normalize numbers and dates with WeText TN first.
- For multiple voices or cloning, choose CosyVoice / ZipVoice.
