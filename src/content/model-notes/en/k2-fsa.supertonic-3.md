## Overview
Supertone's SupertonicTTS 3 is a multi-speaker synthesis model covering 31 languages for international content.

## Use cases
- Multilingual product prompts and narration
- Reading in English, Japanese, Korean, French, and other languages
- Applications needing several preset voices

## Languages and capabilities
- 31 languages, including English, Japanese, Korean, and French
- Multiple preset speakers

## Usage tips
- For Chinese synthesis quality, prefer CosyVoice / MeloTTS.
- Select a voice for the target language before synthesizing.

## Source and license
- Publisher: Supertone · Runtime: sherpa-onnx
- Precision: INT8 · Size: about 250 MB
