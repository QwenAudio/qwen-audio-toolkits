## Overview
CED (Consistent Ensemble Distillation) identifies environmental sounds, events, and scenes, returning audio tags with confidence scores.

## Use cases
- Categorize and search audio content
- Detect applause, laughter, alarms, animal sounds, and other events
- Automatically tag media libraries

## Languages and capabilities
- Language-independent
- Multi-label results using the AudioSet taxonomy

## Usage tips
- Tiny INT8 (about 27 MB) is faster; Small FP32 (about 96 MB) is more accurate.
- Best for clips of about 10 seconds. Segment longer audio.
