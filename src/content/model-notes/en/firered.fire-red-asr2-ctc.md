## Overview
Xiaohongshu's FireRedASR2 CTC covers Chinese, English, and over 20 Chinese dialects. Fast decoding suits long audio and caption production.

## Use cases
- Transcribe dialect content, including Sichuan/Chongqing, Cantonese, Min, and Wu
- Generate captions for long videos
- Large-scale offline transcription

## Languages and capabilities
- Languages: Chinese, English, and 20+ Chinese dialects
- Fast CTC decoding

## Usage tips
- INT8 is about 741 MB; faster and smaller than the AED version.
- Pair with VAD segmentation and punctuation restoration.
