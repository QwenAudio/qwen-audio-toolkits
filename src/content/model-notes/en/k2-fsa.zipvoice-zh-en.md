## Overview
ZipVoice is k2-fsa's flow-matching zero-shot TTS. Its distilled version is trained on Emilia data; upload reference audio and its transcript to clone a voice.

## Use cases
- Chinese and English voice cloning
- Batch voiceovers with a consistent voice
- Personalized offline speech

## Languages and capabilities
- Languages: Chinese and English
- Zero-shot cloning requires reference audio and an accurate transcript

## Usage tips
- Use 3–10 seconds of clean reference audio. Text must match the audio exactly.
- Without reference text, consider PocketTTS (English only).
