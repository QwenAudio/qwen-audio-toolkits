## Overview
Matcha-TTS is a flow-matching Chinese and English model. A separate Vocos vocoder produces natural-sounding 16 kHz audio.

## Use cases
- Chinese and English reading and narration
- Conversational replies with natural intonation
- An alternative for offline Chinese TTS

## Languages and capabilities
- Languages: Chinese and English
- Flow-matching acoustic model + Vocos vocoder

## Usage tips
- Output is 16 kHz. For higher sample rates, consider MeloTTS or CosyVoice.
- Use WeText TN to process numbers and symbols.
