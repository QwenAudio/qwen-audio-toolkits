## Overview
Streaming Paraformer comes in Chinese/English and Chinese/English/Cantonese variants, producing text as you speak for interactive applications.

## Use cases
- Live captions and meeting notes
- Voice conversations and support assistants
- Near-real-time live-stream transcription

## Languages and capabilities
- Trilingual: Chinese, English, Cantonese; bilingual: Chinese, English
- Incremental streaming results and timestamps

## Usage tips
- Choose the trilingual version for Cantonese. Both are about 230 MB.
- For higher Chinese accuracy in offline tasks, consider Paraformer GGUF.
