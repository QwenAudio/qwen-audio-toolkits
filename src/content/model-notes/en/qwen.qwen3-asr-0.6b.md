## Overview
Qwen3-ASR 0.6B is Qwen's high-quality multilingual recognition model. Hotwords help recognize proper nouns such as names and technical terms.

## Use cases
- High-quality offline multilingual transcription
- Terminology-heavy technical, medical, and financial recordings
- Domain-specific applications needing custom hotwords

## Languages and capabilities
- Multilingual recognition
- Hotword enhancement

## Usage tips
- The INT8 version is about 950 MB and may take longer on first load.
- Segment long audio with VAD first.
