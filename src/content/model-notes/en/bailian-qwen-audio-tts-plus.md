## Overview
Model Studio Qwen Audio 3.0 TTS Plus offers high-quality multilingual synthesis, preset voices, and voice cloning, with natural-language instructions to control tone and style.

## Use cases
- High-quality audio content and advertising voiceovers
- Clone a brand voice from reference audio
- Instruction control such as "sound more excited"

## Languages and capabilities
- Chinese and multiple languages
- Preset voices, voice cloning, and natural-language control

## Usage tips
- Higher latency and cost than Flash. Try Flash first for interactive uses.
- For cloning, use a clean, single-speaker sample of about 10 seconds.
