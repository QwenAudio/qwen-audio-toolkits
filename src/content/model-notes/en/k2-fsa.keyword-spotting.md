## Overview
A Zipformer-based streaming keyword detector for Chinese and English. It listens locally for wake words or specified keywords in real time.

## Use cases
- Custom wake words
- Listen for specific terms in meetings or live streams
- Trigger voice commands

## Languages and capabilities
- Languages: Chinese and English
- Streaming detection with custom keyword lists

## Usage tips
- Use keywords of 2–4 characters/words; very short ones may trigger falsely.
- Add RNNoise preprocessing in noisy environments.
