## Overview
Based on pyannote segmentation 3.0, this diarization model detects speaker changes and provides time intervals for each person in multi-speaker audio.

## Use cases
- Identify who spoke when in meetings and interviews
- Segment multi-speaker recordings before transcription
- Speaker-specific podcast captions

## Languages and capabilities
- Language-independent
- Speaker labels and time intervals; no text output

## Usage tips
- Pair with 3D-Speaker embeddings for more stable clustering.
- Specify the speaker count when known to improve accuracy.

## Source and license
- Publisher: k2-fsa (based on pyannote) · Runtime: sherpa-onnx
- Precision: FP32 · Size: about 7 MB · Version: 3.0
