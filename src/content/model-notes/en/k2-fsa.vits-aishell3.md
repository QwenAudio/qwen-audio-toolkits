## Overview
This icefall-trained VITS Chinese multi-speaker model includes 174 AISHELL3 speakers for fast local reading of Chinese text.

## Use cases
- Chinese narration, prompts, and reading
- Compare multiple voices
- Chinese TTS without a network connection

## Languages and capabilities
- Language: Mandarin Chinese
- 174 speakers with adjustable speed

## Usage tips
- Install WeText TN/ITN for numbers, dates, and other text normalization.
- Dataset voices sound relatively plain. For greater naturalness, consider CosyVoice or MeloTTS.
