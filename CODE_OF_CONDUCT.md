# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Contributors should communicate constructively, assume good intent, respect
privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private data or
credentials, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the reporter.
